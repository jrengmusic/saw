## formats

```
@brief saw format strings — excerpt blocks and diff headers.
```

+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| name           | value          | description                                                   |
+================+================+===============================================================+
| excerptReceipt | `# `           | Opens the receipt line for excerpt.                           |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| excerptTargets | `  targets,  ` | Connector after the target count in the receipt line.         |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| excerptFound   | `  found,  `   | Connector after the found count in the receipt line.          |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| excerptMissing | ` missing\n`   | Closes the receipt line with the missing count and a newline. |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| excerptHeader  | `## `          | Opens the block header for excerpt.                           |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| excerptOf      | `  of  `       | Connector between the line range and the line count.          |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| excerptHash    | ` sha256:`     | Connector before the block hash.                              |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| diffOldHeader  | `--- `         | Unified diff old-file header prefix.                          |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| diffNewHeader  | `+++ `         | Unified diff planned new-file header prefix.                  |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| diffPlanned    | ` (planned)\n` | Closes the planned new-file header with a newline.            |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| diffHunkOpen   | `@@ -`         | Opens a unified diff hunk header.                             |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| diffHunkNew    | ` +`           | Starts the planned range of a unified diff hunk header.       |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| diffHunkClose  | ` @@\n`        | Closes a unified diff hunk header with a newline.             |
+----------------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+
